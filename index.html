1. Agile Team Charter

Evidence

Reflection — STAR Method

Situation

During the Velour Essence project, our team needed to establish a clear purpose, vision and structure to guide the development of our custom perfume business. The Agile Team Charter helped us understand what we wanted to achieve and how each team member would contribute towards the project.

Task

My role in the team was Research and Marketing. My responsibility was to research competitors, gather customer feedback and help determine how we would present and promote Velour Essence to our target audience.

Action

I contributed to the team by understanding my responsibilities and how they connected to the overall project objectives. I learned that my role was not only about marketing the product but also about understanding the market, identifying customer needs and ensuring that the business remained relevant to its target audience. I also understood how the different roles worked together, from the Product Owner establishing the vision to the Scrum Master organising the process, the Designer creating the user experience and the Developer building the technical solution.

Result

The Agile Team Charter gave me a better understanding of how an Agile team is structured and why clearly defined responsibilities are important. I learned that successful project development requires collaboration, communication and accountability, as every team member contributes towards a shared goal. This helped me understand the importance of my Research and Marketing role in connecting the business with its customers and supporting the overall success of Velour Essence.
